ET Book

the Bembo-like typeface of Edward Tufte’s books, as an OpenType family

ET Book was designed by Dmitry Krasny, Bonnie Scranton, and Edward Tufte, and used to set The Visual Display of Quantitative Information, Envisioning Information, Visual Explanations, and Beautiful Evidence. This version descends from Daphne Preston-Kendal’s OpenType conversion of the original PostScript fonts, with repaired punctuation, extended language support, and modern font metadata.

Once-lost characters, restored

‡   ‹quoted›   «quoted»

The original PostScript fonts had replaced the double dagger and the single guillemets with extra ligature outlines, so ‡ rendered as “ff” and ‹ › as “ffi” and “ffl”. They are drawn again in every weight and style.
